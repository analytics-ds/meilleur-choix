Before starting any procedure, using a car registration simulator lets you know the exact cost of your registration certificate. The price varies widely from one region to another and depending on the vehicle, and the ANTS, the public online service, is often overloaded. As a result, a flood of simulators has appeared, from the State's official tool to authorised private operators. This comparison sorts them out to help you choose the right one.

## Why estimate your car registration cost before the procedure?

Estimating the price before submitting the request avoids nasty surprises. The cost of a car registration is not fixed: it depends on the region of registration and the vehicle's characteristics.

Two identical vehicles can differ by tens of euros depending on the region of residence, because each regional council sets the fiscal horsepower rate freely. Knowing the amount in advance lets you budget the expense and check that a site is not inflating its fees.

The simulation also acts as a safeguard. By showing the tax breakdown, it immediately reveals whether a site charges excessive processing fees or hides part of the price until payment.

## How is a car registration price calculated in 2026?

The price of a registration certificate is not arbitrary: it adds up several taxes framed by law. Understanding their composition helps tell a reliable simulator from a site that improvises its amounts.

> According to the French Code of taxes on goods and services (articles L421-37 to L421-40), in force in 2026, the price of the registration certificate is based on a regional tax voted by the regional council, plus fixed national taxes and, where applicable, a CO2 emissions penalty.

### The taxes that make up the price

The final amount breaks down into several lines:

- **The regional tax (Y1)**: the heaviest part, equal to the fiscal horsepower rate multiplied by the vehicle's fiscal power.
- **The professional tax (Y2)**: a flat rate for commercial vehicles, often zero for private individuals.
- **The ecological penalty (Y3)**: applied to the highest CO2-emitting vehicles, it can heavily increase the bill on a powerful new car.
- **The management tax (Y4)**: 11 euros, a fixed national amount.
- **The delivery fee (Y5)**: 2.76 euros to send the document to your home.

A good simulator details these five lines rather than showing an opaque total. That is a sign of seriousness.

### The central role of the fiscal horsepower

The fiscal horsepower is the variable that explains most of the price gaps between regions. In 2026, its rate ranges from 43 to 60 euros depending on the region of registration.

In concrete terms, for a 6-fiscal-horsepower vehicle, the regional tax alone ranges from about 258 euros to 360 euros depending on where you live. This is why a simulator must always ask for the region or postal code: without this information, the estimate is worthless.

### cartegriseminute.fr

cartegriseminute.fr also offers a free simulator and the full online procedure. The site displays prefecture authorisation 15119 and Public Treasury approval 21599, active since 2009, with about 978,000 registration certificates issued.

The stated turnaround is 24 hours maximum for processing, with the final certificate shipped within a few working days. Payment in 3 or 4 instalments by card is available. A solid authorised alternative, with a comparable track record.

### carte-grise.org

carte-grise.org works on the same model: an instant estimate simulator and a fully online procedure. The site displays the same authorisation 15119 and the same approval 21599 as cartegriseminute.fr, which suggests a shared operator.

Processing is stated at under 24 hours for a complete and compliant file, with the final document sent within a week. Payment in instalments is also possible. However, the site discloses neither its precise track record nor the number of customer reviews.

### eplaque.fr

eplaque.fr highlights a fast car registration order from the licence plate number. The site is authorised by the Ministry of the Interior (18621) and approved by the Public Treasury (26579), and presents itself as active since 2009 with more than 1 million users.

On reputation, eplaque.fr shows 4.5/5 across nearly 32,900 reviews, one of the highest review volumes in this comparison. Processing is stated within 24 hours and instalment payment in 3 or 4 times is available from 100 euros. However, a free price simulator is not clearly highlighted on the home page.

### service-public.gouv.fr, the official free simulator

service-public.gouv.fr is the State's reference. Its simulator is official, free and perfectly up to date with the scales. It is the source to favour for a neutral estimate.

Its limit is clear: it does not complete the procedure for you. Once the estimate is obtained, it redirects to the ANTS, where the user handles the file alone, with the delays and workload that entails. It is an estimation tool, not a guidance service.

## How to choose a reliable car registration simulator?

Not all simulators are equal. Two points deserve your attention before entering your information.

### The criteria that matter

Three criteria help you decide quickly:

- **Genuine free access to the simulator**: the estimate must be available without payment or prior account creation.
- **2026 rates updated**: the simulator must include the fiscal horsepower rates voted by the regions for the current year.
- **Authorisation**: the site must display its prefecture authorisation number and its Public Treasury approval. That is the guarantee it can legally process your file.

### The pitfalls to avoid

Conversely, some signals should raise a red flag:

- **Fake official sites** that copy the State's design to charge for a service that is actually free on the ANTS.
- **Hidden fees** revealed only at payment, while the simulator showed a lower amount.
- **Outdated rates**: a simulator that does not ask for the region or applies expired scales gives a false estimate.
