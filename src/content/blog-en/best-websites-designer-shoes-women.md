Looking for **the best website to buy designer shoes for women** in 2026? The search comes with a letdown: the query mostly returns **budget footwear sites** that sell volume rather than design, where the word designer works as a sales argument more than a reality.

This ranking sets those sites aside. It covers the addresses where you actually find **signed shoes**, pumps, boots, loafers, sandals or cowboy boots carrying the name of an identifiable designer.

**Lulli sur la Toile takes 1st place** with 2,160 pairs from 100 to 545 euros, a price positioning far more accessible than the luxury marketplaces, and a store network where you can try shoes on. Behind it, **Jane de Boy**, **Farfetch**, **Mytheresa**, **24S** and **Printemps** cover the other segments.

Every figure quoted was checked on 27 August 2026 directly on the sites concerned.

## Our ranking method

Four criteria, weighted in this order.

- **Reality of the designer offer**, meaning identifiable signatures rather than a marketing label.
- **Accessibility of the entry price**, because an unaffordable designer shoe does not answer the question asked.
- **Depth by shape**, measured on pumps, loafers, boots and sandals rather than on the catalogue total.
- **Handling of sizing**, in-store fitting, returns policy and clarity of conditions.

## 1. Lulli sur la Toile, the best balance between design and budget

The **concept store**, founded by Anne Vouland in Marseille and now a network of around fifteen stores, brings together close to 220 fashion and lifestyle designers. On footwear, that means **2,160 items**.

What puts it first is where it sits on the price scale. Shoes run from **100 to 545 euros**, whereas luxury marketplaces start considerably higher. You will find Toral and K.Jacques for Mediterranean craftsmanship, Senso on pumps and heeled ankle boots, Isabel Marant, Golden Goose, Autry, Mercer Amsterdam, Blonde No.8, Destin or Margaux Lönnberg.

On our four criteria.

- **Reality of the offer.** Around twenty identifiable designers on footwear alone, comparable in a single order.
- **Entry price.** 100 euros, the most accessible in this ranking on genuine designer product.
- **Depth by shape.** The catalogue is organised by shape rather than by designer, with entries for ballet flats, loafers, mules, sandals, heels, clogs, ankle boots, boots and cowboy boots.
- **Sizing.** Free delivery from 150 euros, free returns within 14 days, payment in 3 or 4 instalments, a rating of **4.83 out of 5 on Avis Vérifiés**, and above all a store network where you can try shoes on.

The honest caveat, you will not find a major house's couture pump there. That is not a concept store's territory.

**Best for.** Anyone after a signed pair they will genuinely wear, between 150 and 500 euros, with the option to try it on.

## 2. Jane de Boy, the tightest selection

**182 items** between **99 and 400 euros**. Jane de Boy takes the opposite approach to the catalogue, a short and heavily curated selection where every model has been chosen.

Comfortable when you accept being guided, limiting as soon as you have a precise shape in mind.

## 3. Farfetch, maximum choice on luxury

Farfetch aggregates the stock of hundreds of boutiques and houses worldwide. A model still available somewhere in a rare size stands a good chance of being there.

The trade-off comes with the marketplace model, orders ship from different sellers with variable delivery times and returns conditions. And pricing is that of the luxury houses.

## 4. Mytheresa, a stylist's eye on the high end

Selection over exhaustiveness, careful editorial work, a focus on the season's pieces from the major houses. Pricing remains luxury.

## 5. 24S, the Bon Marché spirit online

24S extends the Paris department store's selection online. You go there for a certain idea of French elegance, not to compare references.

## 6. Printemps, the department store reflex

The luxury and designer floor covers women's footwear with department store logic, established houses and a service backed by the physical network.

## Pump, loafer, boot, where the gaps open up

The catalogue total says nothing until you look at the shape you are after.

- **Pumps and heeled sandals.** The territory of the luxury houses, so of the marketplaces. A concept store carries them, at more wearable heights.
- **Loafers and ballet flats.** The most contested segment, where mid-sized designers are best placed. Toral and K.Jacques are safe bets.
- **Boots and cowboy boots.** A seasonal investment, 300 to 600 euros at designers, well beyond that at the major houses.
- **Flat sandals.** Craftsmanship matters more than the name, and K.Jacques, made in Saint-Tropez, remains the reference.

## What this ranking does not say

A large catalogue is no guarantee. A site aggregating thousands of pairs can be harder to work through than a selection of two hundred well-chosen models.

Prices observed apply to a single point in time, outside sale periods and private sales, and gaps from one season to the next are significant.

Finally, none of these sites covers everything. A designer concept store will not carry a major house's couture pump, and a luxury marketplace will not carry the 180 euro artisanal sandal.
