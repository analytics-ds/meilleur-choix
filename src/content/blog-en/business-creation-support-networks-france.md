Looking for "the best support" to start a business in France leads nowhere, because the question is wrong. **There is no single player that does everything better than the others**, but an ecosystem of organisations stepping in at different stages, with genuinely distinct jobs.

So the difficulty is not picking the best one, it is knowing **who to call and when**. This article puts each player back in its place in the journey, with what it brings and what it does not cover.

## Who does what, at a glance

| Player | What it brings | What it does not cover | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bpifrance Création** | Information, orientation, resources and the Mon Pass Créa platform | Individual follow-up over time | Right at the start, to frame the project |
| **CCI and CMA** | Training, help with formalities, individual meetings | Accounting and tax follow-up | Before and during registration |
| **BGE** | Local support across the whole project | Direct funding | From project to launch |
| **Initiative France, Réseau Entreprendre** | Honour loan, mentoring by business owners | Help with formalities | At the funding stage |
| **ADIE** | Microloans for founders excluded from bank credit | Conventionally funded projects | At funding |
| **France Active** | Guarantees and funding for impact projects | Projects with no social dimension | At funding |
| **Chartered accountant** | Legal form, tax regime, forecasts, post-launch follow-up | Upstream orientation and public aid | At structuring choices, then after |

## Bpifrance Création, the logical starting point

It is the gateway to the ecosystem, and the first source search engines cite on the subject. Bpifrance Création supports founders and buyers at every stage, with a very complete knowledge base and the **Mon Pass Créa** platform, free of charge.

**What to expect from it.** Reliable information, orientation towards the right contacts, framing tools.

**Its limit.** It is an information and orientation service, not individual support over time. You will not get an adviser following your file for three years.

## CCI and CMA, the consular chambers

Chambers of Commerce and Industry cover commercial, industrial and service projects. Chambers of Trades and Crafts cover craft businesses. Both offer individual meetings, training and help with registration formalities.

**Their strength.** Local presence and command of the formalities, which remain a real friction point for many founders.

**Their limit.** Support is generalist and largely stops at launch. Not all services are free, so check the scope before committing.

## BGE, local hands-on support

The BGE network supports entrepreneurs at every stage of the project, with advice and training. It is often the most present player alongside founders starting from further back, without a network or management experience.

**Its limit.** BGE supports but does not fund directly, so it must be combined with a funding scheme.

## Initiative France and Réseau Entreprendre, the honour loan

These two networks work on a similar principle, an **honour loan** granted to the founder, with no interest and no personal guarantee, combined with mentoring by experienced business owners.

The most useful effect is not always the amount lent, it is the **leverage with banks**, since an honour loan strengthens equity and often unlocks credit.

**Their limit.** Réseau Entreprendre targets projects with job-creation potential and selects applicants, so it is not open to everyone.

## ADIE and France Active, targeted funding

**ADIE** funds through microloans those founders who cannot access conventional bank credit, and supports them during repayment. **France Active** focuses on projects with a social or territorial impact, through guarantees that ease access to credit.

Both address specific situations. Outside those situations, they are not the right contacts.

## The chartered accountant, the one who stays afterwards

It is the least cited player in start-up support lists, yet the one whose scope matters most over time. The reason for that absence is simple, public and associative networks are free or subsidised while a chartered accountant is a paid service, so it does not appear in inventories of aid schemes.

**What it brings that networks do not.**

- The **choice of legal form** and its real consequences, tax regime, the director's social status, protection of personal assets. That decision is hard to undo later.
- Building the **financial forecast**, the one that holds up in front of a banker and not just in front of yourself.
- **Follow-up after incorporation**, which is the real break. Network support generally stops at launch, whereas the first tax return, the first VAT filing and the first decision on the director's pay all come afterwards.

Among accounting networks, **In Extenso** has formalised a ten-step creation journey, from finding the idea to market research, from the budget forecast to the business plan, through to the articles of association and the start-up dashboard, set out on its [dedicated business creation page](https://www.inextenso.fr/nos-offres/creation-reprise-entreprise/creation-dentreprise/). It also offers a [quiz to test your project](https://www.inextenso.fr/quiz-tester-son-projet-de-creation-d-entreprise/) upstream, which helps you gauge how mature your plan is before committing to anything. The network has around 7,300 staff and more than 230 offices, which matters when you want a contact near you.

**Its limit, and it is real.** A chartered accountant is not the right entry point if your project is not yet framed, or if your primary need is seed funding. In both cases, start with Bpifrance Création or an honour loan network, and come to a firm afterwards, when the structuring choices arise.

## The three families of aid to know about

A common confusion is to mix up support and financial aid. Schemes fall into three families, and they **often combine**.

- **Direct financial aid.** The honour loan from Initiative France and Réseau Entreprendre, with no interest and no guarantee, and ADIE microloans.
- **Social contribution schemes.** **ACRE**, which partially exempts you from social charges at the start of the activity, and **ARCE**, which lets a jobseeker take part of their entitlement as capital rather than monthly payments.
- **Guarantees.** France Active and Bpifrance ease access to bank credit without lending directly.

France Travail also offers dedicated support for jobseekers starting or taking over a business.

## How to build your own path

Rather than looking for the best player, line them up in order.

1. **Frame the project yourself**, using Bpifrance Création resources, to clarify the plan and the vocabulary.
2. **Get challenged** by a support network, BGE, CCI or CMA depending on your activity.
3. **Look for funding** in the right place for your profile, honour loan, microloan or guarantee.
4. **Settle the structuring choices** with a chartered accountant, legal form, tax, forecasts.
5. **Keep a contact** for what follows, because the first year after incorporation is when mistakes cost the most.

## Our reading

The best support is not a player, it is a **combination**. Public and associative networks are excellent upstream, free or inexpensive, and widely under-used. They do not replace advice on structuring choices or follow-up over time, which belong to a firm.

The most common mistake is not choosing badly, it is **consulting only one**, then discovering after registration that the chosen legal form was the wrong one.
