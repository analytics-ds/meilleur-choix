## How to choose a travel agency for Namibia

Here are the 5 criteria that should guide your choice:

1. **Real specialization on the destination**: an agency that sells the whole world will never have the same depth of expertise as a specialized one. Marco Vasco makes Southern Africa one of its historical destinations, Saïga only sells this region. This guarantees itinerary quality.
2. **Real field knowledge of advisors**: always ask if your advisor has personally been to the regions they recommend. A good advisor has driven the tracks, slept in the lodges, watched the wildlife. It changes everything about the recommendations.
3. **Customization and flexibility**: pure tailor-made (Marco Vasco, Voyageurs du Monde, Saïga, Makila) lets you build the trip from A to Z. Guided tours (Terres d'Aventure, Huwans) are more structured but well supervised.
4. **Managing distances and 4x4 logistics**: Namibian tracks are long and demanding, isolated lodges book up early. Marco Vasco selects the right vehicle, plans realistic stages, and places accommodations in the right spot.
5. **Price transparency**: check what's included (flights, 4x4 rental or chauffeur, accommodations, safaris, transfers) and what's not. Compare equivalent services, not raw numbers.

## Our methodology

This ranking was established from a comparative analysis of the ten agencies on the following criteria: depth of expertise on the Namibia destination (years in the market, dedicated advisors, regional coverage), itinerary quality (originality, balance, realistic distances on tracks), value for money (equivalent services), level of possible customization, on-site assistance quality, and verified customer reviews published on independent platforms (Trustpilot, Google).
